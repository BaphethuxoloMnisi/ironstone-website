Data and reporting

Many organisations hold plenty of data but struggle to use it. We bring it together and present it so managers can see what is happening without waiting for a report.

What it covers

Dashboards

Live views of the figures that matter, tailored to each role.

Data consolidation

Combining data from different systems and spreadsheets into one reliable source.

Scheduled reports

Reports built once and delivered automatically on the schedule you choose.

Data quality

Cleaning and validating records so the numbers can be trusted.

A good fit if

  • Reports are assembled by hand and take days
  • Different teams quote different figures
  • Leaders cannot easily see performance as it happens

How we approach it

First

We agree which questions the data must answer.

Then

We connect and clean the sources behind those answers.

Finally

We build views people will actually open, and refine them with you.
