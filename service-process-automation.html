Process automation

Repetitive tasks cost time and invite errors. We automate the routine steps so your people can focus on work that needs judgement.

Custom softwareWebsites and mobile appsCloud and infrastructureData and reportingProcess automationIntegration and securityAll services

What it covers

Approvals and notifications

Requests routed to the right person, with reminders and a clear record.

Data capture

Forms and imports that replace retyping and copy-and-paste.

Reconciliations

Automatic matching of records across systems, flagging only what does not agree.

Document generation

Letters, quotes and reports produced from your data at the click of a button.

A good fit if

  • Staff spend hours on the same task every week
  • Errors creep in when information is moved by hand
  • Approvals stall because nobody knows where a request is

How we approach it

First

We observe the process as it runs today and find the steps worth automating.

Then

We automate in small pieces, starting with the biggest time saver.

Finally

We build in exception handling, so unusual cases reach a person.
