Built for many sectors

Different industries share the same needs: dependable systems, clear data and software people can use. Here is what that looks like in each area we serve.

Financial servicesRetail and commerceHealthcareEducation and trainingLogistics and operationsPublic sector and NGOs

Financial services

Financial organisations handle sensitive data under close scrutiny. Systems must be accurate, traceable and secure.

What these organisations need

  • Smooth, compliant customer onboarding
  • Accurate records and audit trails
  • Timely management reporting

What we build

  • Secure customer portals
  • Client and account management systems
  • Automated reporting and reconciliations

Retail and commerce

Retailers compete on convenience and availability. Customers expect to browse, buy and track orders without friction.

What these organisations need

  • A dependable online store
  • Real-time visibility of stock
  • Orders that flow from checkout to delivery

What we build

  • E-commerce sites and apps
  • Stock and order management
  • Sales dashboards

Healthcare

Healthcare providers need to reduce administrative load while protecting patient privacy.

What these organisations need

  • Easy appointment booking
  • Clear communication with patients
  • Less paperwork for clinical staff

What we build

  • Appointment and reminder systems
  • Patient portals
  • Administrative workflow tools

Education and training

Schools, colleges and training providers need to manage learners and content with limited administrative time.

What these organisations need

  • Organised learner records
  • Easy delivery of course content
  • Visible progress and results

What we build

  • Learner management systems
  • Online course platforms
  • Progress and attendance reporting

Logistics and operations

Operations run on timing. Teams in the field need accurate information on a device they already carry.

What these organisations need

  • Live tracking of jobs and vehicles
  • Efficient scheduling and dispatch
  • Proof of delivery and service

What we build

  • Dispatch and tracking systems
  • Mobile apps for field teams
  • Operational dashboards

Public sector and NGOs

Public bodies and non-profits must deliver services and show accountability, often with tight budgets.

What these organisations need

  • Efficient handling of cases and requests
  • Transparent reporting to funders and the public
  • Systems that stay affordable to run

What we build

  • Case management systems
  • Beneficiary and programme databases
  • Impact reporting dashboards
