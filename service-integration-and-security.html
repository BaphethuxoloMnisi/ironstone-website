Integration and security

Systems work best when they share information, and they must be protected while doing it. We connect your tools and put sensible security around them.

Custom softwareWebsites and mobile appsCloud and infrastructureData and reportingProcess automationIntegration and securityAll services

What it covers

API and system integration

Connecting your accounting, CRM, payment and operational systems so data flows between them.

Access control

Making sure each person sees only what they should.

Data protection

Encryption, secure storage and handling that supports your legal obligations.

Security reviews

A structured look at your systems to find and fix weaknesses.

A good fit if

  • The same information is entered in several places
  • You are unsure who can access what
  • You want an independent check of your current setup

How we approach it

First

We list the systems involved and how information should move between them.

Then

We build integrations that are monitored and fail safely.

Finally

We review security at each stage instead of leaving it to the end.
