How we work

A clear process with defined points where you decide, so nothing arrives as a surprise.

Stage 1

Understand

We learn your goals, users and constraints before proposing anything.

What happens

  • Conversations with the people who will use the system
  • Review of your current tools and data
  • Identification of risks and priorities

What you receive

  • A written summary of findings
  • A shared view of the problem
Stage 2

Plan

Scope, timeline and price are set out in writing for you to approve or change.

What happens

  • Defining what is in and out of scope
  • Choosing the technology and approach
  • Breaking the work into stages with dates

What you receive

  • A project proposal with cost and timeline
  • Clear decision points for you
Stage 3

Build

Work is delivered in short stages so you see real, working progress.

What happens

  • Design and development in short cycles
  • Regular demonstrations of working software
  • Testing throughout, not only at the end

What you receive

  • Working software at each stage
  • Honest progress updates
Stage 4

Support

We launch, train your team and keep the system healthy afterwards.

What happens

  • Careful launch and data migration
  • Training for your team
  • Monitoring, fixes and further development

What you receive

  • Documentation
  • Training and ongoing support, if you want it

Ways to work with us

Choose the arrangement that suits the size and stage of your project. We can combine them.

Fixed project

A defined scope, timeline and price for a specific system.

Ongoing partnership

A monthly arrangement for continued development and support.

Advisory

Independent technical guidance before you commit to a build.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you want to achieve. We will reply with questions or a suggested next step.
