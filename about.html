Company Overview

IRONSTONE (Pty) Ltd was founded in 2026 as a 100% Black youth-owned South African enterprise, built to improve service delivery in the public and private sectors. The company is registered on the National Treasury Central Supplier Database.

We serve government institutions, municipalities, businesses, schools and community organisations through four divisions: IT and software development, civil engineering and construction, office administration and business support, and food and beverage services. We listen first, assign the right specialists, and stay involved after delivery, because we want long-term relationships that support business growth and sustainable development.
