Questions we expect

Straight answers to the things organisations usually ask first.

You are a new company. Why should we trust you?

That is a fair question. We back it with a written scope, staged delivery you can inspect and clear ownership of everything we build, so your risk stays small at every step.

Do you work with small organisations?

Yes. We size solutions to the organisation, and a small team gets the same care as a large one.

Which sectors do you work in?

We are not tied to one industry. We are set up to serve financial services, retail, healthcare, education, logistics and the public sector.

How is pricing decided?

After the first conversation we prepare a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost. You approve it before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A focused website can take weeks, and a larger system is delivered in stages over several months. Your proposal will show the timeline.

Will we own what you build?

Yes. The systems, code and data are yours, and we document them so another team could maintain them.

Do you support systems after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing support and development, but it is optional.

What do you need from us to start?

A conversation about what you are trying to achieve. You do not need a technical specification.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly. We will reply with a clear answer.
